Privacy Policy

App: Motor Soundboard: Exhaust (com.mindskinnerstudio.motorsoundboard)
Developer: Mindskinner Studio
Effective Date: November 20, 2026

1. Overview

Mindskinner Studio respects your privacy. Motor Soundboard: Exhaust does not require account registration and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or microphone audio.

2. Local Storage

Preferences such as your selected engine profile, haptic/limiter settings, 0–100 km/h personal records, and ad-unlock progress are stored strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Analytics

We use third-party services that may collect device identifiers, IP address, and anonymous interaction events to deliver ads and measure app stability:

  • Google AdMob: Used to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded ads. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase Analytics: Used to log anonymous feature interactions (such as engine switches and soundboard triggers) to improve app quality.

4. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact Mindskinner Studio at mindskinnerstudio.dev@gmail.com.
