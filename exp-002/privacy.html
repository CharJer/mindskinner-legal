Privacy Policy

App: FuseBrick: Offline Puzzle (com.mindskinnerstudio.fusebrick) • Developer: Mindskinner Studio • Effective: November 25, 2026

FuseBrick: Offline Puzzle is designed as a 100% offline-first puzzle game. Mindskinner Studio does not require user accounts, logins, cloud profiles, or access to your contacts, camera, microphone, location, or personal files.

1. Local Game Storage

Your board state, high scores, Daily Challenge progress, unlocked themes, discovered perks, and audio/comfort preferences are stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your phone except as described under optional third-party services below.

2. Voluntary Rewarded Ads (Google AdMob & UMP Consent)

FuseBrick contains zero forced banner or interstitial ads. You may optionally choose to watch a voluntary rewarded video ad (marked with an AD icon) for in-run perks, draft rerolls, tool refills, revives, or theme unlocks. When online, the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may process your Advertising ID, IP-derived approximate region, ad interaction events, and consent status to serve and measure ads. Regions covered by GDPR/EEA/UK consent rules receive a UMP consent prompt and an in-app Manage Ad Privacy control inside Settings.

3. Diagnostics & Analytics (Firebase)

When Firebase Analytics is active and online, anonymized app-instance gameplay telemetry (such as session milestones, feature usage, and crash/performance signals) helps us fix bugs and balance puzzle difficulty. No personal name, email, or user-generated content is collected.

4. Local Notifications

If you opt in to Smart Reminders, FuseBrick schedules strictly local, offline Android reminders on your device with quiet hours (10:00 PM to 9:00 AM). You can disable Smart Reminders anytime inside Settings.

5. Data Deletion & Reset

  • In-App Wipe: Open Settings → Erase All Saved Data to permanently delete all local scores, streaks, unlocked themes, and perk history.
  • Uninstall: Uninstalling FuseBrick removes all app-local storage from your device.

6. Children's Privacy (13+ Audience)

FuseBrick: Offline Puzzle is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Contact

Questions or privacy requests? Contact Mindskinner Studio at mindskinnerstudio.dev@gmail.com.
